# Product & Food Photography

Recipes for shooting (or prompting) products, food, beverages, and beauty items. Each entry combines vocabulary glossary terms into a composable formula.

---

## Food Photography

### Hero Shot (The Plate)

**The brief**: The primary image of a finished dish — the "money shot" for a menu, cookbook, or Instagram post.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **3/4 angle** (45 degrees) or **overhead / bird's eye** depending on the dish's height
- Lighting: **soft directional side light** (window light or large softbox from one side, reflector opposite for fill) — creates dimension on the food without harsh shadows
- Depth of field: **moderate shallow** (f/2.8-f/4) for 3/4 angle — sharp on the hero dish, background softens. **Deep** (f/5.6-f/8) for overhead — everything sharp
- Color: **warm color grade** — slightly amber, appetite-enhancing. Never cool/blue
- Composition: **negative space** on one side for text overlay if editorial. **Rule of thirds** placement for the hero dish
- Surface: **matte wood**, **dark slate**, **marble**, or **linen** — the surface texture frames the food

**Example prompt**: Overhead food photography, perfectly plated pasta dish on dark slate surface, soft directional window light from left, warm amber color grade, shallow depth of field, fresh herbs garnish, negative space upper third for text, editorial cookbook style

**Variations**:
- Swap overhead → 3/4 angle for dishes with height (burgers, layered cakes, tall drinks)
- Swap dark surface → white marble for bright/airy brunch aesthetic
- Swap warm grade → slightly desaturated for modern minimalist food editorial
- Add steam/rising heat for hot dishes (signals freshness)

**Common mistakes**: Cool/blue color grading kills appetite. Too many props compete with the dish. Direct hard flash flattens food texture.

### Dark / Moody Food

**The brief**: Rich, dramatic food imagery — the "Rembrandt of food." Dark backgrounds, warm highlights, chiaroscuro on the plate.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **3/4 angle** (shows depth and dimension)
- Lighting: **hard side light** or **Rembrandt-style** from one direction, **no fill** or **negative fill** (black card opposite to deepen shadows)
- Depth of field: **shallow** (f/1.8-f/2.8) — hero element sharp, everything else falls off
- Color: **warm highlights, crushed blacks** — deep shadows, amber/gold on lit surfaces
- Surface: **dark wood**, **dark stone**, **cast iron** — absorbs light, low reflectivity
- Atmosphere: **moody, intimate** — a single candle or focused beam quality

**Example prompt**: Dark moody food photography, artisan bread on dark wood surface, hard directional side light from right, deep shadows with no fill, warm amber highlights, crushed blacks, shallow depth of field, Rembrandt lighting quality, dramatic chiaroscuro

**Variations**:
- Add visible steam or smoke for hot dishes — catches the side light dramatically
- Swap bread → steak for the classic dark-and-dramatic protein shot
- Add one warm practical light (candle) in background for depth

**Common mistakes**: Going too dark — the food must still be appetizing. Crushed blacks on the food itself hide detail.

### Bright / Airy Food

**The brief**: Light, fresh, Instagram-friendly. The brunch aesthetic. Clean, minimal, uplifting.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **overhead** (flat lay) or **eye-level** for drinks
- Lighting: **overcast / diffused daylight** — even, soft, no harsh shadows. **High-key**
- Depth of field: **deep** (f/5.6-f/11) — everything sharp and clean
- Color: **bright, slightly overexposed**, minimal color grading — let the food's natural color speak
- Surface: **white marble**, **light wood**, **white linen** — bright, reflective surfaces
- Props: minimal — fresh herbs, clean utensils, one accent color

**Example prompt**: Bright airy overhead food photography, acai bowl on white marble surface, soft diffused daylight, high key, minimal shadows, pastel toppings, fresh berries, negative space, clean minimal composition, Instagram brunch aesthetic

**Variations**:
- Add a hand entering frame (reaching for the dish) for UGC/casual feel
- Swap white marble → pastel ceramic plate for color accent
- Slight Portra 400 film grade for warm vintage brunch feel

### Action / Pour Shot

**The brief**: Frozen motion — sauce dripping, cheese pulling, honey pouring, cocktail splashing. The "moment" shot.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **eye level** or **slight low angle** (to see the pour/drip in profile)
- Lighting: **hard backlight** (to illuminate the liquid/drip from behind — makes it glow) + **fill from front**
- Shutter: **fast shutter / frozen motion** — every droplet sharp
- Depth of field: **moderate shallow** (f/4) — the action is sharp, background soft
- Color: **warm** for honey/chocolate, **cool** for water/cocktails — match the liquid's temperature

**Example prompt**: Food action shot, honey dripping from wooden dipper onto pancake stack, hard backlight making honey glow golden, frozen motion droplets, slight low angle, warm amber color, shallow depth of field, dark background, professional food photography

**Variations**:
- Cheese pull (pizza, burger) — slower motion, strings stretching, warm backlight
- Cocktail splash — fast freeze, hard light, dark background, vivid liquid color
- Pour-over coffee — steam + liquid stream, warm practical lighting

### Ingredient Flat Lay

**The brief**: Overhead grid of raw ingredients — the "before cooking" shot. Organized, graphic, educational.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **overhead / bird's eye** (directly above)
- Lighting: **soft even diffused light** — no shadows, even illumination edge to edge
- Depth of field: **deep** (f/8+) — everything equally sharp
- Composition: **organized grid** or **artful scatter** — each ingredient in its own space
- Color: **accurate, natural** — the ingredients' own color is the palette
- Surface: **single-tone background** — wood, marble, or solid color that doesn't compete

**Example prompt**: Overhead ingredient flat lay, organized grid of fresh vegetables and herbs on butcher block wood, soft even diffused daylight, deep focus, accurate natural color, each ingredient separated, clean minimal composition, cookbook editorial style

## Beverage Photography

### Cocktail / Wine Hero

**The brief**: A single drink as the star. Elegant, luminous, often backlit.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **eye level** (to see the liquid level, color, and garnish in profile)
- Lighting: **backlight** (the liquid glows from behind — transparent liquids become luminous) + **subtle fill from front** to show glass detail. **Strip light** from one side for specular highlight on glass edge
- Depth of field: **shallow** (f/2-f/2.8) — glass sharp, background dissolved into bokeh (bar lights become circles)
- Color: **match the drink** — warm amber for whiskey, vivid for cocktails, cool for clear spirits
- Surface: **dark bar surface** or **wet reflective surface** — doubles the glass in reflection
- Detail: **condensation droplets** on glass, **ice clarity**, garnish texture

**Example prompt**: Cocktail photography, negroni in crystal rocks glass, backlit making the orange-red liquid glow, condensation droplets on glass, strip light edge highlight, dark bar surface with wet reflection, shallow depth of field, warm bokeh circles from bar lights behind, eye level

**Variations**:
- Wine glass — side light catching the legs/tears, deep red or golden color
- Coffee — overhead for latte art, steam rising, warm tones
- Water/clear spirits — blue-cool backlight, ice detail, ultra-clean

### Bottle / Can Product Shot

**The brief**: The product packaging as hero — label visible, material quality showcased.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **eye level** or **slight low angle** (bottle appears taller, more imposing)
- Lighting: **two strip lights** on either side (creates clean gradient highlights on curved surface) + **backlight** (for glow-through if glass). **No front light** — the gradients define the form
- Depth of field: **moderate** (f/5.6) — label sharp, slight softening at edges
- Color: **match brand colors** — the product dictates the palette
- Surface: **reflective** (shows product reflection) or **gradient background** (smooth transition behind)

**Example prompt**: Product photography, craft beer bottle on reflective black surface, two strip lights creating gradient highlights on glass, label fully visible and sharp, slight backlight for amber glow through beer, dark gradient background, eye level, commercial beverage photography

## Hard Goods / Product

### Hero Product on Surface

**The brief**: The product centered, beautifully lit, on a surface with reflection. The "launch image."

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **eye level** or **3/4 above** depending on product shape
- Lighting: **softbox key** from one side + **rim/edge light** from behind for separation + **subtle fill**. For reflective products: **tent lighting** (enclosed diffusion) or precise strip lights
- Depth of field: **moderate** (f/4-f/8) — product sharp, slight background falloff
- Color: **clean, accurate** — the product's actual colors must read correctly
- Surface: **gradient surface** (light to dark sweep) or **material surface** (concrete, marble, wood) matching brand identity
- Post: **minimal** — product photography demands accuracy

**Example prompt**: Product photography, wireless headphones on dark gradient surface, soft key light from left, rim light separating product from background, subtle reflection on surface, clean accurate color, moderate depth of field, commercial hero shot

**Variations**:
- Floating/levitating product — composited, dramatic rim light, no surface, tech/aspirational
- Lifestyle context — product in use (on a desk, in a hand, in a room), environmental lighting
- Multiple products — arranged in a family, consistent lighting across all items

### E-Commerce White Background

**The brief**: Clean, even, accurate product on pure white. Amazon, Shopify, catalog standard.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **front**, **3/4**, and **side** (typically all three shots per product)
- Lighting: **light tent / tent lighting** — enclosed diffusion, shadowless, even. Or: **two large softboxes** flanking + **background lit separately** to pure white
- Depth of field: **deep** (f/8-f/11) — everything sharp
- Color: **100% accurate** — this is for purchase decisions, not mood
- Background: **pure white** (#FFFFFF) — no gradients, no shadows, no texture

**Example prompt**: E-commerce product photography, running shoe on pure white background, even shadowless lighting from all sides, accurate color, deep focus, every detail sharp, clean commercial catalog style, no shadows

## Beauty / Cosmetics

### Packaging Hero

**The brief**: The bottle, tube, or compact as luxury object. Specular highlights, glossy surfaces, aspirational.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **eye level** or **slight low angle** (product appears monumental)
- Lighting: **strip lights** for specular highlights on glossy surfaces + **controlled backlight** for glow-through (if translucent). **Beauty dish** or **softbox** as fill
- Depth of field: **shallow** (f/2.8) — logo sharp, rest falls off beautifully
- Color: **brand palette** — luxury brands lean warm gold, clinical brands lean cool white
- Surface: **wet/glossy surface** (for reflection) or **textured stone** (for natural brands)

**Example prompt**: Beauty product photography, luxury serum bottle on wet black surface, strip light creating a bright specular highlight on glass, soft backlight for golden glow through liquid, shallow depth of field, warm luxurious color, reflection in wet surface, commercial beauty hero

---

### Swatch / Texture Macro

**The brief**: The product's color and texture at extreme close-up — lipstick swatches, foundation shades, cream texture.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **overhead** or **extreme close-up / macro**
- Lighting: **soft even diffused** — accurate color reproduction with no specular hotspots
- Depth of field: **shallow macro** for texture (paper-thin focus) or **deep** for flat swatch grid
- Color: **100% accurate** — this is color matching, not mood
- Surface: **clean skin** (for on-skin swatches) or **white/neutral card** (for product swatches)

**Example prompt**: Beauty macro photography, three lipstick swatches on forearm, soft diffused even lighting, accurate color reproduction, shallow macro depth of field showing creamy texture, clean skin, product color swatches, commercial beauty
