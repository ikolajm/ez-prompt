# Marketing & Advertising

Recipes for commercial creative — hero visuals, brand photography systems, and ad formats. The key distinction: marketing imagery serves a business objective. Every visual choice supports the brand strategy.

---

## Hero / Key Visual

The primary campaign image. The one shot that represents the entire campaign across all touchpoints.

### Lifestyle Hero

**The brief**: Aspirational scene with the product in context. The viewer imagines themselves in this world.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **85mm f/2** — flattering compression, subject isolation, aspirational depth
- Lighting: **golden hour** or **soft directional natural light** — warm, inviting, real-world feeling
- Angle: **eye level or slight low** — the viewer is present in the scene, or looking up slightly (aspiration)
- Color: **warm, brand-aligned grade** — the palette supports the brand's emotional territory
- Composition: **subject in environment** — product visible but not centered. The world matters as much as the product
- **Negative space** for headline/copy placement — the image must work WITH text overlay

**Example prompt**: Lifestyle marketing hero, person using wireless earbuds while running through sunlit park, 85mm shallow depth of field, golden hour warm backlight, brand-aligned warm color grade, negative space in upper third for headline, aspirational active lifestyle, campaign key visual

**Variations**:
- Tech brand → swap park for modern office/city, swap golden hour for clean soft even light, cool palette
- Wellness brand → swap running for yoga/meditation, soft overcast, muted earth tones
- Luxury brand → swap casual for formal setting, medium format for richer tonality, Hasselblad quality

---

### Product-Focused Hero

**The brief**: The product IS the hero. Clean, dramatic, the product commands attention.

**The recipe**:
- Lighting: **dramatic product lighting** — rim light for separation, controlled key for dimension, gradient on background
- Angle: **3/4 above** or **eye level** — shows the product's best angle
- Depth of field: **shallow to moderate** (f/2.8-f/5.6) — product sharp, background falls away
- Color: **brand colors** dominate — the product and background are color-coordinated
- Composition: **centered or slightly off-center** with negative space for copy
- Background: **gradient**, **solid color**, or **minimal environmental context**

**Example prompt**: Product-focused marketing hero, smartphone floating at 3/4 angle, dramatic rim light and soft key, dark gradient background matching brand navy, product sharp with slight background bokeh, negative space for headline, premium tech campaign

---

### Conceptual / Abstract Hero

**The brief**: Mood over product. The feeling of the brand without showing the product directly. Art direction territory.

**The recipe**:
- Style: reference an **art movement** or **visual aesthetic** aligned with brand values
- Lighting: **dramatic, stylized** — this is where creative lighting choices pay off
- Color: **brand palette pushed to extreme** — the color IS the message
- Composition: **abstract or symbolic** — shapes, textures, patterns that evoke the brand
- Product: **absent or barely visible** — the concept is the hero

**Example prompt**: Conceptual brand hero, abstract flowing silk in brand coral and navy, dramatic side lighting, silk catching light mid-flow, no product visible, rich saturated color, editorial art direction, brand world key visual

## Brand Photography Systems

Consistent visual language across all touchpoints — website, social, print, packaging. The system is more important than any single image.

### Warm / Approachable Brand (Wellness, Food, Lifestyle)

**The system**:
- Lighting: **soft natural** — golden hour, overcast, window light. Never hard/dramatic
- Color: **warm muted** — earth tones, cream, sage, dusty rose. Portra 400 aesthetic
- Texture: **natural materials** — wood, linen, ceramic, plants
- Composition: **open, breathing** — negative space, uncluttered, intimate
- People: **natural, unposed** — real expressions, casual, approachable

---

### Cool / Premium Brand (Tech, Finance, Automotive)

**The system**:
- Lighting: **clean, controlled** — soft even studio light or blue hour. Precise, not warm
- Color: **cool neutral** — navy, silver, white, charcoal. Minimal warm tones
- Texture: **engineered materials** — brushed metal, glass, carbon fiber, clean surfaces
- Composition: **geometric, precise** — centered, symmetrical, Swiss-influenced grid
- People: **composed, confident** — the subject is in control

---

### Bold / Disruptive Brand (Startup, Streetwear, Gen Z)

**The system**:
- Lighting: **direct flash** or **neon/colored** — raw, energetic, not polished
- Color: **vivid, clashing** — neon accents, bold primaries, Memphis-influenced
- Texture: **urban** — concrete, neon, screens, stickers
- Composition: **dynamic, asymmetric** — diagonal, tight crop, unconventional framing
- People: **expressive, active** — energy over elegance

## Ad Formats

### Feed Ad (Facebook/Instagram, 1:1 or 4:5)

**The brief**: Paid creative that competes in a feed of organic content. Must stop the scroll while looking native enough not to trigger "ad blindness."

**The recipe**:
- First 20% of image (top): **the hook** — the visual element that stops the scroll
- Color: **high contrast against typical feed content** — bolder than organic posts
- Subject: **single clear focal point** — one product, one face, one scene
- Text overlay: **minimal** — platforms penalize text-heavy images. Let the visual do the work
- Aspect: **4:5** for maximum screen real estate on mobile

---

### Story / Vertical Ad (9:16)

**The brief**: Full-screen vertical between organic stories. Must feel native to the story format.

**The recipe**:
- Motion: **implied or actual** — static images feel wrong in the story context. At minimum, subtle parallax or text animation
- Text zones: **middle third** safe — top and bottom occupied by platform UI
- Style: **match organic story quality** — not too polished, not too raw. The "elevated UGC" zone
- CTA: **swipe up / link area at bottom** — visual must lead the eye downward
