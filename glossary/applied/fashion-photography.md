# Fashion Photography

Three distinct sub-genres with completely different visual rules. The editorial/commercial/lookbook distinction is the core insight: editorial breaks rules, commercial follows them, lookbook is systematic.

---

## Editorial Fashion

The artistic end. Storytelling, provocation, art direction. For magazine spreads, galleries, and creative portfolios. The clothing is a character in a narrative, not a product for sale.

### High-Concept Editorial

**The brief**: A fashion story with a concept — surreal sets, dramatic lighting, narrative through imagery. The Tim Walker / Guy Bourdin territory.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **medium format** (Hasselblad, Pentax 67) for rich tonal depth, or **35mm** for raw energy
- Lighting: **dramatic single source** — Rembrandt, split, or hard side light. Shadows are part of the story
- Angle: **unconventional** — low angle for power, Dutch angle for tension, overhead for pattern
- Color: **highly graded** — pushed to extreme. Saturated, desaturated, cross-processed, or monochromatic depending on the concept
- Composition: **full body or environmental** — the set/location IS the story alongside the clothing
- Style ref: **Tim Walker** (fantastical sets), **Guy Bourdin** (surreal narrative), **Steven Meisel** (dark provocation)

**Example prompt**: High-concept fashion editorial, model in flowing red gown on a surreal oversized chess board set, dramatic Rembrandt side lighting, deep shadows, medium format rich tonality, bold saturated color, Tim Walker fantastical production, full body environmental shot

**Variations**:
- Dark/provocative editorial → swap Tim Walker for Helmut Newton — hard light, B&W, architectural setting, power pose
- Avant-garde → swap conventional beauty for Comme des Garçons sculptural garments, unconventional model, deconstructed fashion

---

### Gritty Street Editorial

**The brief**: Fashion meets documentary. The anti-studio approach — real streets, harsh light, raw energy. Juergen Teller, Terry Richardson territory.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **35mm** (Leica, Contax T2) or even **disposable camera** — the small format = casual energy
- Lighting: **direct on-camera flash** (overrides ambient, harsh and flat) OR **hard midday sun** (no diffusion, strong shadows)
- Angle: **eye level, slightly off-center** — snapshot framing, not composed
- Color: **punchy and slightly wrong** — cross-processed, oversaturated, or desaturated grit
- Film stock: **Kodak Gold** or **disposable camera aesthetic** — warm, grainy, imperfect
- Composition: **tight, slightly claustrophobic** — heads cut off, limbs exiting frame, unconventional crops

**Example prompt**: Gritty street fashion editorial, model in designer coat on urban sidewalk, direct on-camera flash at night, harsh flat lighting, snapshot framing, head slightly cropped, Kodak Gold warm grain, Juergen Teller anti-glamour, raw and imperfect

**Variations**:
- Swap flash for hard midday sun — same raw energy but outdoor/daytime
- Swap urban for beach/motel — Richardson territory
- Add motion blur for kinetic energy

---

### Beauty Editorial Close-Up

**The brief**: Face and skin as the canvas. Makeup, skin texture, and light as the subject. The beauty dish + clamshell territory.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **medium format** or high-resolution digital for skin detail
- Lighting: **butterfly / clamshell** (beauty dish overhead + reflector below chin) — the standard beauty setup. Even, flattering, minimal shadows. Dual catchlights in eyes
- Angle: **straight-on eye level** — the face fills the frame
- Depth of field: **moderate shallow** (f/4) — eyes sharp, ears and hair softening
- Color: **clean, accurate skin tones** — minimal grading. The makeup IS the color
- Composition: **tight close-up or extreme close-up** — face filling frame, sometimes cropped at forehead

**Example prompt**: Beauty editorial close-up, model with bold graphic eye makeup, clamshell lighting with beauty dish overhead and silver reflector below chin, dual catchlights in eyes, clean accurate skin tone, tight crop at forehead, medium format shallow depth of field, dewy skin texture, high-end beauty campaign

**Variations**:
- Swap beauty dish for ring light — flatter, more social media/contemporary
- Swap clean grade for monochrome/B&W — Peter Lindbergh raw beauty territory
- Add wet/dewy skin for luminous texture

## Commercial Fashion

The garment is the product. The goal: make the clothing look desirable and purchasable. Flattering light, clean framing, the garment visible.

### Studio Lookbook

**The brief**: Consistent, clean studio shots showing the full garment. The backbone of any fashion brand's catalog.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **medium format** digital (Hasselblad, Phase One) for maximum detail and accurate color
- Lighting: **three-point** or **soft key + fill** — even, flattering, the garment reads clearly. No dramatic shadows hiding detail
- Angle: **eye level**, model centered or slightly off-center
- Depth of field: **moderate** (f/5.6-f/8) — model sharp front to back
- Color: **accurate, minimal grading** — the fabric color must be true for purchase decisions
- Background: **seamless white**, **light gray**, or **brand-colored sweep**
- Composition: **full body** or **3/4** — the entire outfit must be visible

**Example prompt**: Commercial fashion lookbook, model standing in neutral pose wearing tailored navy blazer and cream trousers, three-point studio lighting on white seamless background, even flattering illumination, accurate fabric color, full body framing, clean professional, e-commerce catalog style

---

### E-Commerce Flat Lay

**The brief**: The garment laid flat, no model. Overhead shot. For online stores.

**The recipe**:
- Angle: **overhead / bird's eye** (directly above)
- Lighting: **even diffused** — shadowless or minimal shadow. The garment must read as flat, clean, accurate
- Depth of field: **deep** (f/8+) — everything equally sharp
- Color: **100% accurate** — purchase decision depends on color truth
- Background: **pure white** or **off-white**
- Styling: garment pressed, steamed, arranged to show shape. Minimal accessories

**Example prompt**: Fashion e-commerce flat lay, denim jacket arranged on white background, overhead shot, even shadowless diffused lighting, accurate indigo denim color, sleeves slightly angled to show shape, deep focus, clean commercial

---

### Lifestyle Commercial

**The brief**: The garment in an aspirational real-world context. On-model in a beautiful location. The "brand world" image.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **85mm f/1.8-f/2.8** — flattering compression, subject isolation from environment
- Lighting: **golden hour** natural light or **soft overcast** — warm, flattering, the environment is visible
- Angle: **3/4 body** or **full body** — outfit visible within context
- Color: **warm, slightly graded** — the Portra 400 aesthetic. Romantic, aspirational
- Location: **brand-appropriate** — beach for resort, city for urban, countryside for heritage
- Composition: **environmental portrait** — the subject occupies 1/3 to 1/2 of the frame, the setting tells the brand story

**Example prompt**: Lifestyle fashion photography, woman in linen sundress walking through lavender field at golden hour, 85mm shallow depth of field, warm Portra 400 color grade, soft natural backlight, full body with environment visible, aspirational summer campaign

**Variations**:
- Urban brand → swap lavender field for city rooftop at blue hour, swap warm for teal/orange grade
- Streetwear → swap 85mm for wide angle, swap golden hour for direct flash at night, raw energy

## Campaign / Seasonal

### Resort / Cruise Campaign

**The brief**: Warm-weather collection. Aspirational travel. Light, color, paradise.

**The recipe**:
- Lighting: **golden hour** or **bright midday sun** (for that "vacation" quality)
- Color: **warm, saturated** — turquoise water, golden sand, vivid fabrics
- Location: **beach, pool, Mediterranean architecture, tropical foliage**
- Mood: **relaxed, joyful, sensual** — the model is on vacation
- Camera: **medium format** for richness or **35mm** for casual energy

**Example prompt**: Resort fashion campaign, model in flowing printed maxi dress by turquoise infinity pool, bright warm Mediterranean sunlight, saturated vivid color, lush tropical foliage, relaxed joyful expression, medium format rich tonality, luxury resort, aspirational travel

---

### Fall / Winter Campaign

**The brief**: Cool-weather collection. Layered, textured, moody. Warmth comes from the clothing and the light, not the weather.

**The recipe**:
- Lighting: **overcast soft** or **warm window light** — subdued but dimensional
- Color: **warm but muted** — earth tones, burgundy, forest green, camel, charcoal
- Location: **autumnal landscape**, **city in rain**, **wood-paneled interior**, **stone architecture**
- Mood: **contemplative, sophisticated, cozy** — the clothing protects against the cold
- Texture: **visible fabric texture** — wool, tweed, cashmere, leather, knit patterns visible

**Example prompt**: Fall fashion campaign, model in camel wool overcoat and burgundy scarf, overcast soft light on autumn city street, muted warm earth tones, visible wool texture, contemplative expression, wet pavement reflections, slightly desaturated, editorial sophistication
