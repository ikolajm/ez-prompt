# Social Media Formats

Recipes organized by platform and format. The aspect ratio is the primary constraint — it dictates composition, text placement, and visual hierarchy. The same subject needs different recipes for different platforms.

---

## Instagram

### Feed Post (1:1 or 4:5)

#### Curated Grid Aesthetic

**The brief**: A cohesive Instagram grid where every post shares a visual language — consistent color, consistent composition, recognizable as one brand.

**The recipe**:
- Color: **one consistent grade** across all posts — warm Portra, cool desaturated, pastel, or brand-specific palette
- Composition: **consistent framing rules** — always centered, always rule-of-thirds, always with same negative space placement
- Lighting: **consistent quality** — always soft natural, always direct flash, always golden hour
- Aspect: **4:5** preferred (takes more screen real estate than 1:1 in feed)

**Example prompt**: Instagram feed aesthetic, [subject], soft diffused natural light, warm muted color grade with slight film grain, centered composition with negative space, 4:5 vertical crop, cohesive minimal style, consistent with grid aesthetic

---

#### Thumb-Stopping Single Image

**The brief**: A single post designed to stop the scroll. Maximum visual impact in a fraction of a second.

**The recipe**:
- Color: **high contrast, bold** — complementary colors, vivid saturation. The image must pop against surrounding posts
- Composition: **clear single subject** centered or at power points — no ambiguity about what to look at
- Lighting: **dramatic** — Rembrandt, hard side light, rim light — anything that creates dimension and contrast
- Element: **one surprising/unusual element** — unexpected color, scale play, visual illusion

**Example prompt**: Thumb-stopping Instagram image, bold red subject against teal background, complementary color contrast, dramatic side lighting, single clear focal point, high saturation, 4:5 vertical, designed to stop the scroll

---

### Carousel (1:1 or 4:5, swipeable)

#### Educational Carousel

**The brief**: Information delivered in swipeable slides — tips, how-tos, listicles. Each slide is a designed graphic, not a photograph.

**The recipe**:
- Style: **flat design / Swiss typographic** — clean, readable, type-driven
- Color: **brand palette**, **2-3 colors max** per slide, consistent across all slides
- Typography: **bold sans-serif** headlines, clean body text, **hierarchy through size** not decoration
- Layout: **consistent template** — same placement of headline, body, page number across all slides
- Slide 1 (cover): **hook headline** + bold visual — the only slide that sells the carousel
- Final slide: **CTA** — follow, save, share

**Example prompt**: Instagram educational carousel slide, clean flat design, bold sans-serif headline "5 Lighting Tips", teal and white brand colors, minimal layout with strong type hierarchy, slide 3 of 7, consistent template, professional graphic design

### Stories / Reels (9:16 Vertical)

#### UGC Style (User Generated Content)

**The brief**: Looks authentic, unproduced, "real." The anti-professional aesthetic that paradoxically requires thought to execute well.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **smartphone** or smartphone-simulated — computational HDR, deep focus, slightly wide angle
- Lighting: **ring light** (the selfie standard) or **available/ambient light** — natural, imperfect
- Angle: **eye-level direct address** or **handheld POV** (first-person showing hands doing something)
- Color: **minimal grading** — authentic, not cinematic. Slight warmth at most
- Framing: **selfie-distance close-up** or **over-the-shoulder POV**
- Audio: implied — the visual suggests someone is talking to camera

**Example prompt**: UGC-style Instagram Reel frame, person holding product to camera, ring light catchlights in eyes, smartphone-quality slightly wide angle, natural imperfect framing, authentic feel, 9:16 vertical, not over-produced, casual direct address

**Common mistakes**: Over-producing UGC defeats the purpose. Studio lighting, color grading, and perfect composition make it look like an ad pretending to be organic.

---

#### Polished Vertical Video

**The brief**: Cinematic quality but vertical. The "elevated Reel" for brands with higher production value.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **shallow depth of field** (f/1.8-f/2.8) — the main differentiator from phone footage
- Lighting: **soft directional** — one key light, subtle fill, not flat
- Angle: **gimbal-stabilized movement** or **static with subject motion**
- Color: **cinematic grade** — teal/orange, warm vintage, or brand-specific
- Composition: **subject in center third** with text-safe zones top and bottom (Instagram overlays UI there)
- Aspect: **9:16** — the full vertical frame

**Example prompt**: Polished Instagram Reel frame, fashion model walking toward camera, shallow depth of field, soft directional key light, warm cinematic color grade, gimbal-smooth camera movement, 9:16 vertical, text-safe space top and bottom, elevated brand content

## TikTok

### Trending Native Aesthetic

**The brief**: Fits the TikTok visual language — direct address, ring light, eye-level, personality over production.

**The recipe**:
- Lighting: **ring light** — the defining TikTok light source. Circular catchlights in eyes
- Angle: **eye-level**, camera at arm's length or on tripod, **straight-on direct address**
- Color: **bright, clean** — TikTok's algorithm favors well-lit, clear footage
- Background: **clean/simple** — bedroom, solid wall, or blurred environment
- Framing: **head and shoulders**, close enough for facial expression to read on small screens

**Example prompt**: TikTok talking-head frame, person at eye level, ring light catchlights, bright even lighting, clean simple background, head and shoulders framing, direct address to camera, 9:16 vertical, authentic personality

---

### ASMR / Texture Content

**The brief**: Extreme close-up tactile content — the satisfying crunch, the smooth pour, the texture reveal. Sensory-driven.

**The recipe**:
- Camera: **macro lens** or macro mode — extreme close-up
- Lighting: **soft diffused** — even, no distracting highlights. The texture IS the visual
- Angle: **overhead** or **extreme close-up profile**
- Depth of field: **very shallow** — paper-thin focus zone, rest melts away
- Sound: implied — the visual is designed for audio accompaniment (crunch, squish, pour)
- Pace: **slow, deliberate** — let the texture fill the frame

**Example prompt**: ASMR texture content, extreme macro close-up of thick paint being mixed with palette knife, soft diffused even lighting, very shallow depth of field, cream and pigment texture filling frame, satisfying tactile visual, 9:16 vertical, slow deliberate

## YouTube

### Thumbnail (16:9, 1280x720)

**The brief**: 3-5 seconds to convince someone to click. Maximum information at minimum size. Must read as a tiny image in a grid of competing thumbnails.

**The recipe**:
- Face: **extreme expression** — surprise, excitement, concern. Facial expression is the #1 click driver
- Color: **high saturation, bold contrast** — must pop at thumbnail size. Complementary colors
- Text: **3-5 words maximum** in bold, high-contrast type with outline or shadow for legibility
- Composition: **face on one side, text/subject on the other** — the "split" layout. Or face with text overlaid
- Background: **simple, bold color** or **blurred context** — don't compete with face and text
- Avoid: small text, muted colors, complex compositions, anything that doesn't read at 150px wide

**Example prompt**: YouTube thumbnail, person with surprised expression on left, bold red and yellow background, 3 words in thick white outlined text on right, high saturation, complementary colors, simple bold composition, reads at small size, 16:9

## Pinterest

### Aspirational Lifestyle Pin (2:3 Vertical)

**The brief**: Beautiful, save-worthy, aspirational. Pinterest users save things they want to recreate — the image must inspire action.

**The recipe**:
- Lighting: **soft natural** — golden hour, window light, overcast. Warm and inviting
- Color: **warm, slightly desaturated** — the Portra/Pinterest palette. Cream, sage, dusty rose, warm wood
- Composition: **2:3 vertical** with room for text overlay if editorial. **Lifestyle context** visible
- Style: **cottagecore, minimalist, Scandinavian** — the Pinterest aesthetic skews warm/natural/aspirational
- Subject: **actionable** — a recipe, an outfit, a room, a project. Something the viewer can DO

**Example prompt**: Pinterest lifestyle pin, woman in linen apron arranging wildflowers on wooden table, soft golden hour window light, warm muted Portra color palette, cream and sage tones, 2:3 vertical composition, negative space for text overlay, aspirational cottagecore, save-worthy
